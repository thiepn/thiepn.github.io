Method

The app is built around productive difficulty.

01

Attempt before reveal

Parsing, vocabulary, and reading always start with retrieval rather than passive recognition.

02

Hints escalate gradually

Tap a word once for a grammatical cue, twice for the lemma, and only then for the full parse.

03

Read real text early

Grammar exists to unlock Scripture. The reading workspace is not an afterthought at the end of the course.
