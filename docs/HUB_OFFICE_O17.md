O17 addresses a distinct missing original-source long-document workflow: safe whole-subtree duplication and heading hierarchy promotion/demotion. Existing O9 could move sections and O16 could insert blank sibling/child headings, but neither could duplicate a full selected section with contents or change the heading hierarchy of its descendants without manual text surgery.

New src/lib/office/section-transform.ts derives exact original section boundaries from documentSections and real code-fence-aware outlineFor; duplicates a complete subtree without rewriting original content, or promotes/demotes all authentic nested headings by one level while preserving every other source byte. Heading levels must stay in 1..6; demotion requires an earlier same-parent sibling. Missing/stale selections, invalid depths and >200,000-character output are rejected atomically by the pure transform. Source and historical records are immutable during preparation.

The existing Office outline adds three preview actions (Duplicate, Promote, Demote). Their owner-local in-memory preflight contains the entire read-only resulting Markdown, selected original section, title, identity scope, ID, current revision and exact saved body. Buttons require a saved, unconflicted document. Confirmation opens an explicit dialog and calls exactly one existing OfficeDeviceStore.save(current,title,result) CAS transaction; the editor changes only AFTER success. CAS rejection leaves the original textarea intact, marks unsafe writes blocked and preserves original backup/export paths. No duplicate editor, schema, store, sync writer or new history authority.
