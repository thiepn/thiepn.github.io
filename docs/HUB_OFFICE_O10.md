O10 provides explicit local comparison of original stored historical revision with currently open Office Markdown source: latest/current title, historical title, changed lines (safe textContent, no HTML interpretation), first 80 differing line pairs, bounded counts and truncation. Historical source is the original immutable OfficeDocument.history array; no shadow index, diff DB, extra revision writer or synthesized IDs. User chooses 1–200 consecutive historical lines, maximum 40,000 characters, previews exact text, then must separately confirm either insertion at editor cursor or replacement of a nonempty current text selection. All replacement/insertion is bounded to the original 200,000-character document maximum. Active Account owner, document ID, original revision and historical revision are rechecked; stale comparisons close. The edit is issued only through the original textarea input event and original CAS autosave, creating a new revision while preserving old snapshots subject to original MAX_HISTORY=12 retention. Errors and conflicting edits do not mutate original histories. Full-document "Restore selected" remains the existing original historical restore workflow.

Comparison and excerpt DOM/text clear upon privacy/Account changes, document switches or source revision changes, with controls disabled during busy/conflicted states. New Vitest covers bounded comparisons, unchanged prefix/suffix, excerpt validation and source-only insertion/replacement; Playwright covers exact recovery, history retention, invalid selections and mobile privacy. No Account/KEEP/Manuscript/StudyOS/Folio/Hub v1 data reads/writes or new Office storage. Draft only; no merge, deploy, migrations, production writes, credentials or costs.
