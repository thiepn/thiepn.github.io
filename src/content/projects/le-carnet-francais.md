A broader French course companion that combines lesson progression, grammar explanation, practice, and review.

The notebook model gives grammar, examples, exercises, and review a single progression path. It is broader than a flashcard deck: the learner can move from a construction's meaning into repeated use and then return to it later as part of the same course companion.
