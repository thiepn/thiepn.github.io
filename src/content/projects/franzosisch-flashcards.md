A compact vocabulary-first French flashcard experience designed for quick review without the broader course structure of the other French tools.

This artifact deliberately keeps a narrower scope than the larger French systems in the catalogue. It is for rapid vocabulary retrieval: compact prompts, quick repetition, and local review when a full lesson environment would be unnecessary.
