Unreached is a browser-based Christian world atlas built around the loop Explore → Understand → Pray.

It connects geographic exploration with people groups, language and Scripture information, contextual explanations, evidence-aware methodology, and prayer guidance. The project deliberately keeps uncertain or legally gated mission data visible as a limitation rather than filling gaps with fabricated certainty.
