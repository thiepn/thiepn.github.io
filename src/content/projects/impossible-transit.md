Impossible Transit is a first-person 3D surreal transit game built entirely for the browser.

The journey begins with Service 00 and unfolds through stations where scale, gravity, recursion, architecture, and eventually the transit network itself become unreliable. Its visual and mechanical identity comes from transforming familiar transit spaces into increasingly impossible spatial systems.
