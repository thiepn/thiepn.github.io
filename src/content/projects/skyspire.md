A vertical platforming game focused on upward momentum, precise movement, and the tension of preserving hard-won height.

Progress is measured physically through height, which makes every successful movement valuable and every mistake costly. The game therefore depends on stable character control, readable vertical routing, and a run structure that can sustain pressure beyond the opening minutes.
