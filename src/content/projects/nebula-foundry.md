A long-form incremental game about production, expansion, singularities, and progressively deeper automation systems.

The early loop is intentionally simple, but the system expands through generators, upgrades, prestige layers, and automation. Its long-form design is about the feeling of a manual production line gradually becoming a self-running cosmic machine.
