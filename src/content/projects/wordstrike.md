A fast arcade typing game where precision and reaction speed directly control survival, score, and combo flow.

Campaign, Typing Test, Endless, and Daily Strike share typing as the input language but create different forms of pressure. In the arcade modes, whole-word targets move toward a central core, so recognition, target selection, accuracy, and speed all affect the run.
