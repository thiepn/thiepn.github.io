An experimental incremental game that turns mathematical themes into progression systems and escalating numerical mechanics.

Mathematical language becomes the theme and structure of an incremental progression loop rather than a conventional lesson. The experiment explores how automation, scaling, prestige, and accelerating numerical systems can make abstract analysis motifs feel like game mechanics.
