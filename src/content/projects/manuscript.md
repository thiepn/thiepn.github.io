A local-first Markdown publishing environment focused on structured writing, document production, and durable project workflows.

Its central transition is from structured source to composed publication. Markdown remains the durable authoring layer, while the output side resolves hierarchy, typography, figures, and document structure into something intended to be read and distributed.
