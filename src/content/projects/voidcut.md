VOIDCUT is a precision arcade game about cutting away space while protecting moving nodes.

Its identity comes from making every swipe consequential: the arena changes shape, safe space contracts, and clean geometry becomes both the player's weapon and the primary source of risk. Deterministic runs turn that spatial mechanic into a repeatable mastery challenge.
