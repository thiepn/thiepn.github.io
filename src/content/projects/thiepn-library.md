The THIEPN Library is the publishing and reading platform behind the public book catalogue: a static-first system for books, research editions, PDFs, EPUBs, search, and native browser reading.

Its role in the portfolio is distinct from the individual books themselves. Books are publications; the Library is the software and release system that discovers, renders, verifies, and serves them.
