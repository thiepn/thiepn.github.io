Artikelwerk is a German noun-gender trainer aimed at advanced vocabulary, where article recall becomes harder because the words themselves are less familiar. The project keeps the practice loop deliberately narrow: see a noun, choose its article, get immediate feedback, and continue.

The app is being developed as a dedicated practice environment rather than a long reference page, with vocabulary support and responsive interaction intended to keep repeated sessions fast on both phones and desktops.
