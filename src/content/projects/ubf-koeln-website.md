UBF Köln Website V2 is a stakeholder prototype exploring how the church's public website could make first-visit information and core navigation substantially easier to reach. It prioritizes practical questions such as where the church is, when worship happens, what visitors should expect, and how to explore events or resources.

The prototype is intentionally a decision tool rather than a final production replacement. It gives stakeholders a complete responsive experience to compare against the current site and separates improvements in information architecture and user flow from purely visual changes.
